# Netball Unleashed 0.8.5-beta: the run, for review

Rendered from the game's own code (`netball-game/tests/gait_view.tscn`), Low and Medium graphics. A player sets off,
jogs, sprints at 6.8 m/s and stops on a floor marked every 0.5 m (the `.mp4`s); the `.png`s are contact sheets of a
stride (jog, sprint), stopping, and the umpire's jog. Feet stay planted on the floor lines; arms bent and swinging.
(Medium here is the fallback without the licensed clips, as local builds have no clips.)
