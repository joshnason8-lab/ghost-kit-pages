Kabaddi Raid

Raid. Touch. Return.

A 3D kabaddi game for Android. Cross the baulk line on one breath, touch a defender and get back over the midline before they bring you down; or defend with ankle, thigh and waist holds and chain tackles.

Google Play: coming soon Android beta (APK) Play in your browser

Three ways to play

Quick Match

Two teams, one ground, play now: twelve franchises and twelve countries, five grounds from a village mat to a stadium.

League Season

Own a franchise. Keep your best players, win the auction, pick your seven, and take them through the league, the playoffs and the final, season after season.

Nations Cup

Pick a country and its squad of twelve, name a captain, and go from the groups to the final.

Real kabaddi

  • Hand touches, toe and back kicks, the dubki under linked arms, the lion jump over a dive.
  • Ankle, thigh and waist holds, dashes and chain tackles, with team-mates coming in to help.
  • The rules as they are played: bonus points, revivals, all outs, do-or-die raids, super tackles, the golden raid and time outs, with referees signalling every call.
  • Eight languages: English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Punjabi.
  • No ads, no purchases, no account. It plays offline and collects nothing (privacy policy).
