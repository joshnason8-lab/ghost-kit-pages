Kabaddi Raid Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Kabaddi Raid is a kabaddi game for Android phones and tablets, published by Josh Nason. This policy explains what the game does with your information. It is short, because the game does very little with it.

The summary. Kabaddi Raid does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There is no account to make, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The Android app does not even have permission to use the internet: everything you do in the game stays on your phone.

What the game stores, and where

The game keeps these in its own private storage on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • Your settings: language, sound, vibration, graphics level, camera, controls and difficulty.
  • Your saved games: your League Season (your franchise, its squad, results, news, awards and records) and your Nations Cup.

Other apps cannot read them, and they are not sent anywhere. They are not included in your phone's cloud backup either. Uninstalling the game deletes them all; updating it keeps them.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission: vibration, so the phone can buzz at a tackle or a point. You can turn it off in the game's Settings. It does not ask to use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Links that open your browser

Settings has links to this page, the game's support page and its website. Tapping one opens your phone's web browser; the game itself sends nothing. The page you open is covered by the policy of the site it is on.

The browser version

A version of the game also runs in a web browser. That page is served by Netlify, which keeps standard server logs (such as your IP address and browser type) as described in Netlify's privacy policy. The game sends nothing to us there either; its settings and saved games are kept in your browser's storage on your device.

Children

Kabaddi Raid is a sports game suitable for players of all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. If the game ever starts to collect any data, this page will say so before the update that does it is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the game, can be sent through our contact page: thekabaddipulse.com/contact.
