Khamba Dash

Kho kho for Android: sit in the lane, give the kho, chase to the pole and dive. The classic rules of the national championships and the fast rules of the world championship, twenty-four nations, eighteen states and a league of eight invented city sides, coach tips for your first match, four grounds from a village maidan to an indoor arena, in fourteen languages of India, Nepal, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka.

Khamba Dash is an independent game, not affiliated with or endorsed by any kho kho federation, league, club or player. The players are fictional.
