Khamba Dash · Kho kho for Android

Help

Playing

  • Chasing: you are always the active chaser, ringed in gold. The joystick (left thumb, anywhere on the left) runs him; touch a defender and he is out. Run up behind a seated chaser who faces the other side, and when his square lights up press Kho: you become him, rising on the far side of the lane. Dive throws you full length at a defender just out of reach; in the free zone behind a pole it is a pole dive.
  • The direction rule: once you set off toward one pole you may not turn back or cross the lane, except by going round a pole. With Settings > Lane assist on (the default) the game keeps you to it; off, the umpire whistles you.
  • Defending: you run one of the batch of three; Switch takes another and Dodge is a quick burst. Stay behind the active chaser, where he cannot turn, and inside the lines.
  • Too quick? Choose the Beginner difficulty and three-minute turns in the match setup.

The rules

The game plays the national federation's rules (Classic: nine a side, 1 point an out, the follow-on) and the seven-a-side game of the 2025 world championship (Fast: 2 points an out, dream runs, the wazir and the power play). How to Play in the game has the whole of it.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Budget phones start on Low by themselves, and the game lowers it on its own if a match runs slowly.
  • Words in your language look wrong: please tell us which screen and what it says; the translations are new and we want them right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size, Move buttons to drag them where your thumb is, and Left-handed to swap the sides.
