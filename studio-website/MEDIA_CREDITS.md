# Media credits

Every photo on the website's sport pages, with its author and licence. All come from Wikimedia Commons under licences that allow reuse; each is credited under the photo on its page too. This file is written by `tools/build_content.py`; edit the sport's `content-src/sports/<sport>.md` instead.

## The games' own pictures

The icons, title art and screenshots on the site come from the games themselves (each game's `store/graphics/` folder on its branch). On the sport pages they are labelled *In-game*.
