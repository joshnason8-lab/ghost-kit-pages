# Sepak Takraw Unleashed privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Sepak Takraw Unleashed is a sepak takraw game for Android phones, made by Josh Nason (World Stage Games). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The same policy is in the game, under Settings > About > Privacy policy, and at worldstagegames.com/games/sepak-takraw-unleashed/privacy/.

## The summary
- The game is free to play and shows adverts. The adverts come from Google AdMob.
- There is no account and no sign-in. We do not run a server, and we do not collect, see or keep your personal data.
- Google, which serves the adverts, collects some information from your phone to show and measure them. In Europe, the UK and places with similar laws, the game asks for your choice first, and you can change it at any time.
- You can pay once to remove the full-screen adverts, and you can buy coin packs. Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

## What the game keeps on your phone
- Your settings: language, graphics, sound, controls and difficulty.
- Your progress: coins, the things you have unlocked in the shop, your league seasons and cups, daily goals and your record book.
- Whether you have bought "Remove ads".

These are small files in the game's own private storage on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Adverts (Google AdMob)
The game shows full-screen adverts at breaks, for example after a match, never during play. You can also choose to watch a video for coins.

The adverts are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google. To show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's advertising software in the game collects information such as:
- your phone's advertising ID;
- your IP address, which can show your approximate location (city or country, not your exact position);
- information about your phone and the app, such as its model, operating system, language and the game's version;
- how you interact with the adverts, such as which were shown and whether you tapped one;
- diagnostics, such as crash and performance information from the advert software.

Google may use this to show adverts that suit your interests (personalised adverts) where you have agreed to that or the law allows it, and otherwise shows non-personalised adverts. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy:
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive this information, and we do not sell any information about you.

## Your choices about adverts
- Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and places with similar laws, the game shows Google's consent message the first time you play, before any advert is requested. Your choice is stored on your phone.
- Changing your choice: where the law gives you this choice, Settings > About > Privacy choices opens the message again, and you can change your answer at any time.
- United States: where US state privacy laws apply, the same message lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for advertising.
- Your advertising ID: you can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID, or opt out of personalised adverts, in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- Fewer adverts: "Remove ads" in the shop turns off the full-screen adverts for good. Videos for coins stay available, because you choose to watch them.

## Purchases (Google Play)
"Remove ads" and the coin packs are bought through Google Play's own payment screen. Google Play handles the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. It tells the game only which item you bought, so the game can unlock it. We never see your name, e-mail address or payment details. "Remove ads" is restored automatically if you reinstall the game on a phone signed in to the same Google account.

## Permissions
The game asks Android for these permissions:
- Internet and network state: to load adverts and to reach Google Play for purchases. The game itself does not need the internet to play.
- Advertising ID: used by Google's advert software, as described above.
- Google Play billing and licence check: for purchases, and to check the game was installed from Google Play.
- Vibration: to buzz the phone when your player touches the ball. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Keeping the screen awake: so the screen does not turn off during a match.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, or your exact location.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us below.

## Deleting your information
We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To delete what the game keeps on your phone, uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings. To manage information held by Google, use your phone's ad settings and Google's controls at https://myaccount.google.com.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at worldstagegames.com/games/sepak-takraw-unleashed/privacy/ and in the game, with a new date at the top.
