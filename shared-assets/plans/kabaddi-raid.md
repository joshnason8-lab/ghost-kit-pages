# Kabaddi Raid: graphics plan

India. Grounds: Mumbai Dome, Gaon Maidan (village mud court), National Stadium (floodlit), Monsoon Ground (rain),
Puri Beach (sand).

## Gaon Maidan

- **Sky:** `skies/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky`. **Court:** `surfaces/brown_mud_dry`, with `red_dirt_mud_01` around it.
- **Trees:** `trees/rain_tree` and `rain_tree_card`, `trees/coconut_palm`, `trees/island_tree_a_card` and
  `island_tree_b_card`; `foliage/dry_grass_tall_card` at the edges.
- **People's things:** rows of `seating/monobloc_chair`, `seating/folding_stool`, `props/scorers_table` at the
  midline for the scorers' table; huts in your own geometry with `clay_plaster` and `clay_roof_tiles` or
  `thatch_roof_angled`.

## Monsoon Ground

- **Sky:** `skies/overcast_soil_puresky`. **Ground:** `surfaces/brown_mud` (wet), with puddles using
  `water/water_normal_512.png` and a sky-coloured reflection.
- Darker rain trees; `seating/monobloc_chair` under tarpaulins.

## Puri Beach

- **Sky:** `skies/qwantani_sunset_puresky` or `table_mountain_1_puresky`. **Sand:** `surfaces/coast_sand_01`, and
  `damp_beach_sand` toward the water.
- **The sea** behind the court: a wide water plane with `water/water_normal_512.png` and a gentle swell.
- `trees/coconut_palm` and `coconut_palm_card` along the beach, `seating/monobloc_chair`.

## National Stadium and Mumbai Dome

- **National Stadium:** `skies/kloppenheim_07_puresky` (night over a city), `stands/bleacher_concrete`,
  `buildings/city_apartments` on the skyline.
- **Mumbai Dome:** `stands/bleacher_metal` for any lower tier you rebuild.

## Gap

**A temple:** a village shrine at Gaon Maidan, and the Jagannath temple's tower on the skyline at Puri Beach (Puri's
landmark). Neither free library has one. Note it in PROGRESS; the lead will ask the owner.

## Not for this game

Thai temple, stilt house, oak, terraced houses, the thatched cottage, jacaranda.
