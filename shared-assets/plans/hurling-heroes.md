# Hurling Heroes: graphics plan

Ireland. Green, grey skies, hedgerows, stone walls, terraced towns. **No palms, no temples, no tropical trees.**

## Parish field at dusk

- **Sky:** `skies/belfast_sunset_puresky` (a real Northern Irish sunset).
- **Pitch:** `surfaces/grass_ground` (mown) with mowing stripes in your shader; `sparse_grass` along the touchlines
  and `withered_grass` in the worn goalmouths and the 13 m area.
- **Surrounds:** hedgerows of `foliage/grass_wispy_card`, `foliage/fern_card` and `foliage/grass_clumps_card`;
  `trees/oak_tree`, `trees/broadleaf_tree_dark` and `trees/small_broadleaf_card` behind; `props/mossy_rocks` along a
  dry-stone wall; `buildings/thatched_cottage` and a `buildings/terrace_row` for the village beyond.
- **At the ground:** `seating/park_bench` by the clubhouse, `seating/painted_bench` in the dugouts, a small
  `stands/bleacher_concrete` terrace, `props/street_lamp_tall` on the road.
- **Water (optional):** many Irish clubs sit by a lake or the sea: a strip of water beyond the far hedge, using
  `water/water_normal_512.png`.

## County ground

- **By day:** `skies/suburban_football_field` or `skies/kloofendal_overcast_puresky` (Irish grey).
  **Under lights:** `skies/kloppenheim_07_puresky`.
- `stands/bleacher_concrete` terraces, `buildings/terrace_row` around the ground (county grounds sit in towns),
  `props/street_lamp_tall` outside.

## National stadium

- `stands/bleacher_metal` and `bleacher_concrete` for the lower tiers; `buildings/terrace_row` on the skyline
  beyond the stands. The national stadium in Dublin is surrounded by terraced streets, so this is accurate.

## Goals

The owner's `Hurling.Goal` upload is a soccer goal (no uprights above the bar), so it isn't used. Keep your own posts
built to the rules.

## Not for this game

Palms, temples, monobloc chairs, rain trees, Southeast Asian or Indian buildings, jacaranda.
