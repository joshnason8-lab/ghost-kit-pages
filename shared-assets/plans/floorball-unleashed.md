# Floorball Unleashed: graphics plan

Floorball is played indoors: Sweden, Finland, Switzerland, Czechia and their neighbours. Halls are bright, clean and
Nordic: pale wood, white walls, good light.

## The sports hall (birch walls)

- **Walls:** pale birch slats (`surfaces/white_maple_veneer`, tinted slightly warmer) above painted block
  (`concrete_block_wall` normal, white or pale grey paint); a strip of high windows.
- **Floor:** keep your synthetic floor colour; add `surfaces/rubber_tiles` (or `rubberized_track`) as a subtle grain
  and normal so it reads as a real sports floor, not a flat colour.
- **Ceiling:** trusses and rows of `hall/fluorescent_light`.
- **Basketball backboards folded up to the ceiling** at each end, clean and regulation (build them: clear glass
  1.80 × 1.05 m board, orange 45 cm rim, a white net; folded on a steel winch frame), as in every Nordic hall.
- **Small things:** `hall/wall_clock`, `hall/fire_alarm` by the doors, green exit signs, `hall/wet_floor_sign` by the
  entrance, `seating/gym_bench` along the walls behind the substitution zones, the penalty benches and secretariat
  table you already have.

## The school gym (wall bars)

- Older and smaller: `concrete_block_wall` painted pale, `surfaces/wood_floor` or maple, `hall/hanging_lamp` rows,
  wall bars and climbing ropes (yours), `seating/gym_bench` (the classic low gym bench), `hall/wall_clock`, a clean
  regulation basketball board on the end wall.

## The city arena

- `stands/bleacher_metal` for the lower tiers; keep your crowd. `skies/gym_01_light_1k.hdr` for floor reflections.

## Not for this game

Anything outdoors-only: trees, palms, temples, houses. If the halls get windows, show a pale sky only.
