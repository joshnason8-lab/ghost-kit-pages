# Kho Kho Rush: graphics plan

India (with Nepal, Bangladesh and Sri Lanka). Red and brown earth, rain trees and coconut palms, plastic chairs,
plastered and tiled houses.

## Village ground at dusk

- **Sky:** `skies/qwantani_dusk_2_puresky` (warm dusk).
- **Ground:** `surfaces/red_laterite_soil_stones` or `brown_mud_dry` for the beaten earth, worn along the central
  lane and round the poles as you already do.
- **Trees:** `trees/rain_tree` (near) and `rain_tree_card` (far), `trees/coconut_palm`, `trees/island_tree_a_card` and
  `island_tree_b_card`; `foliage/dry_grass_tall_card` and `grass_clumps_card` at the edges.
- **Houses:** keep yours, retextured with `surfaces/clay_plaster` and `yellow_plaster` walls and `clay_roof_tiles`
  or `thatch_roof_angled` roofs.
- **People's things:** rows of `seating/monobloc_chair` (white plastic chairs are what spectators sit on at local
  matches), `seating/folding_stool`, `props/scorers_table` for the scorer and timekeeper.

## School ground by day

- **Sky:** `skies/qwantani_noon_puresky` (hot, clear). **Ground:** `dry_ground_01` and `brown_mud_dry`, with
  `sparse_grass` at the edges.
- **School:** walls in `plastered_wall` or `yellow_plaster`, `clay_roof_tiles`; `seating/painted_bench` for the
  pupils; `buildings/city_apartments` behind the wall in a town.

## Indoor arena on mats

- `stands/bleacher_metal`, `hall/fluorescent_light` rows, `surfaces/rubber_tiles` as the mat's grain under your
  colours.

## Floodlit stadium (red earth)

- **Sky:** `skies/kloppenheim_02_puresky`. **Ground:** `terrain_red_01` or `red_laterite_soil_stones`.
- `stands/bleacher_concrete` for the stand, `buildings/city_apartments` on the skyline.

## Gap

**A small village temple or shrine** would make the village ground truly Indian. Neither free library has one. Note
it in PROGRESS; the lead will ask the owner about generating one.

## Not for this game

Thai temple, stilt house, jacaranda, oak, terraced houses, the thatched cottage (it's English and Irish).
