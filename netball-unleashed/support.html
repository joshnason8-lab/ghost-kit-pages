Help

Playing

  • Your player has the lime ring at her feet. The joystick (your left thumb, anywhere on the left of the screen) runs her. Lead into space to get free for a pass.
  • With the ball she may not run: that is footwork. Push the joystick toward a team-mate to pick her (a ring shows who), then Pass, or Lob over a defender. The ring over the ball is your three seconds.
  • Shooting: Shoot appears when your Goal Shooter or Goal Attack has the ball in the circle.
  • Defending: Defend puts her arms up. Keep 0.9 m from the landing foot of the player with the ball (the ring round it turns red when you are closer) or the umpire calls obstruction. Intercept leaps for a pass.
  • Team or one position: on the home screen, Team lets you play whoever has the ball; a bib (GS to GK) lets you play that position all match.

The rules

The game plays the Rules of Netball as they stand (2024 edition): seven a side, each in her areas; the centre pass alternating after every goal; footwork, three seconds, obstruction at 0.9 m, contact, offside, over a third, short pass; free passes, penalty passes and the penalty shot; four quarters, and extra time to a two-goal lead when a match must have a winner. How to Play in the game has all of it.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Budget phones start on Low by themselves.
  • Words in your language look wrong: please tell us which screen and what it says; the translations are new and we want them right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size and Move buttons, and Left-handed to swap the sides.
