# The beta: installing and testing

## Install

1. On the phone, open https://github.com/joshnason8-lab/ghost-kit-pages/releases/tag/centre-pass-netball-beta.
2. Tap `centre-pass-netball-<version>.apk` under Assets.
3. Open it when it has downloaded. If Android asks, allow your browser (or Files) to install unknown apps.

Each new beta installs over the last and keeps your saves. It runs on 64-bit Android phones (every phone sold since
about 2019).

## New in 0.2.0-beta

- **The Club Season** (home screen, under the Nations Cup): pick one of eight clubs, play seven rounds, then the
  semi-finals and the grand final.
- **A coach for your first match**: a short tip the first time you need each thing. It turns off after your first
  full match; Settings > Coach's tips turns it back on.
- **Umpires**: a warned player who infringes again has the pass escalated or advanced; a player suspended twice is
  ordered off (replaced after four minutes). Settings > Umpiring: fewer whistles, standard, or the real rate.
- **Fixes from playtesting**: the pause button shows; Back closes the language list before leaving; the language
  list fits every screen; the interval shows each team's figures and who would come on; name tags no longer print
  over each other; your pass target stays steady and avoids passes a defender can reach.

## What to try

- **The controls**: with the ball, push the stick toward a team-mate (a ring shows who) and Pass or Lob within your
  three seconds (the ring over the ball). Shoot from the circle with your GS or GA. In defence, Defend from 0.9 m
  (the ring round the landing foot turns red when you are too close) and Intercept a pass.
- **Team or one bib**: on the home screen, pick Team, or a bib to play one position all match. Does the bib you
  chose feel like that position?
- **The rules**: is every call right? Offside (try Settings > Keep me onside > Off), held ball, obstruction, the
  centre pass alternating, the penalty player standing out of play.
- **Scrolling**: drag any long page (How to Play, Settings, the cup) from anywhere on it, its words included.
- **Move buttons** (Settings, or the pause menu): drag the buttons where your thumb finds them.
- **The Nations Cup**: pools, semi-finals, the bronze match and the final.
- **The Club Season**: does the table, the finals draw and the grand final make sense?
- **A first match** (or Settings > Coach's tips on): did the tips teach you the game within a minute?
- **Your language**: every screen. Note any word that is wrong, cut off or too long.
- **A budget phone**: does it stay smooth on Low? Does the phone get warm? (Settings > Frame rate > Battery saver.)
