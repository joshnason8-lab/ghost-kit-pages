# The beta: installing and testing

## Install

1. On the phone, open https://github.com/joshnason8-lab/ghost-kit-pages/releases/tag/netball-unleashed-beta.
2. Tap `netball-unleashed-<version>.apk` under Assets.
3. Open it when it has downloaded. If Android asks, allow your browser (or Files) to install unknown apps.

Each new beta installs over the last and keeps your saves. It runs on 64-bit Android phones (every phone sold since
about 2019).

## New in 0.9.10-beta

- **Throw-ins**: the umpire on that line now steps off to stand beside the thrower, not on top of her.
- **Club Season on Low**: the scoreboard's club crests cost less to draw, so a club match on Low is back within the
  game's own drawing budget (the crests look the same).
- The injury stoppage's note reads "A player from England is hurt" (it said "A England player").

## New in 0.9.9-beta

- Players' feet stay planted more reliably as they land, stop and turn (the shared animation foundation's latest
  fixes).

## New in 0.9.8-beta

- **Positional changes**: besides substitutions, two of your players on court can now swap positions, at the
  intervals, in an injury stoppage or a time-out, and after a goal (Pause, then Team changes). Tap Positions beside
  Substitutions and pick a pair, say GS and GA; tap it again to change your mind. Each player can only move to a
  position she plays. The swap is made as play restarts, and each player then keeps to her new position's areas.
- The new words are in every language the game has, awaiting native speakers' checks.
- The store listings in Afrikaans, isiZulu, Kiswahili, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese, Sinhala and Tamil now describe the Club
  Season and the coach's tips, as the English ones do.

## New in 0.9.7-beta

- **Home and away**: a Club Season can now be fourteen rounds, every club played at home and away (pick Single or
  Home and away when you start one). Single is still seven rounds.
- **A second chance for the top two**: the finals are the top-four series netball leagues play. First v second in
  the major semi-final (the winner goes straight to the grand final), third v fourth in the minor semi-final (the
  loser is out), then the preliminary final, then the grand final. A season you started before keeps its old finals.
- In every language the game has; the new words await native speakers' checks.
- **A change after a goal**: as the 2024 rules allow, either team may make a substitution after a goal, before the
  next centre pass (Pause, then Team changes); the AI uses it for a hurt player.
- **The obstruction signal**: the umpire shows it properly, both arms out in front, one hand over the other.
- **The bench**: the reserves sit on their benches, and stand to clap their team's goals (Medium and High). The
  sports hall's benches are now low school benches.

## New in 0.9.6-beta

- **Fairer umpiring**: a call is made only when the infringement really happens (an obstruction once she is inside
  0.9 m, offside once she is over the line); advantage is played once, and the same contact again is called.
- **Centre pass**: the thrower may not be first to touch her own pass; the umpire calls it.
- **Rebounds and loose balls**: players reach for the ball and it comes into their hands, never jumping into them; a
  pass nobody reaches bounces, slows and is run down by the nearest players.
- **Nobody gets stuck**: players walk round a goal circle they may not enter, and your player stays on the court
  in every venue even with Keep me onside off.
- Smoother feet on Low.

## New in 0.9.5-beta

- **A full house in the arena**: the bowl is stepped rows of seats with real people in every seat; the rows nearest
  the court are full 3D fans who turn to the ball and clap (Medium and High), the far tiers are person-shaped
  cut-outs. No painted crowd anywhere. The empty rows of the courtside stands show as blue seats.
- **Room at penalties**: the penalised player stands 1.3 to 1.6 m beside the thrower, never in front of her, and
  walks round behind her to get there; the thrower waits for her.
- Every note you have given us is now a test the game must pass before a beta goes out.

## New in 0.9.4-beta

- **Solid props**: nobody walks through a table, bench, board, sign or post, and the ball bounces off them (each
  at its own bounce: the post's pad soft, the boards hard).
- **Defenders' arms**: a defender over the ball lifts both arms up toward it with soft elbows, never held straight
  out to the sides; running into a defence or changing moves no longer flings an arm out straight.
- **Shooters face the post**: a shooter who catches facing away turns to the post before she shoots.
- **Smoother on Medium and High**: only the players nearest the camera are drawn in full detail (14 on Medium, 22
  on High); the reserves and far players use the lighter model.
- Fixed: after a throw-in over the goal line beyond the circle, the player taking it could stand at the circle's
  edge for good and the match never restarted; she now goes round it. The player taking a restart waits for a
  tossed ball to come down instead of taking it out of the air.

## New in 0.9.3-beta

- **Passes come down**: a pass nobody reaches carries on along its arc and drops to the floor under gravity, then
  bounces and rolls; a whistle in mid-pass lets the ball fall (it never hangs in the air), and a held ball stays in
  the player's hands. Long passes fly higher and a little slower, as real ones do.
- **The net really moves**: a goal balloons the rope net round the ball and swings it about 25 cm before it
  settles; a ball off the ring sways it too. Visible on every camera.
- **Room at penalties and obstructions**: the penalised player stands about 1.3 m away, beside the thrower (never
  in front of her), and every other defender keeps at least 0.9 m from the ball; the umpire waits until they have.
  In open play the defender marking the ball keeps her 0.9 m too.

## New in 0.9.2-beta

- **The stick goes exactly where you push it, diagonals too**: pushing up-right now moves your player up-right on
  the screen from every camera (before, diagonals drifted 15 to 20 degrees off).

## New in 0.9.1-beta

- **A loading screen when you tap Start**: the screen dims at once with a moving bar, the buttons switch off so a

## New in 0.9.0-beta

- **Real ball flight**: every pass and shot flies under real gravity; lobs float high and hang for over a second (and
  the receiver leaps to take them), chest passes are quick and flat, shots arc into the ring.
- **Advantage**: when the umpire plays on after contact, the defender steps out of the way.
- **The crowd sits and stands properly** in every venue, on every level: seated fans in their seats, standing fans

## What to try

- **The controls**: with the ball, push the stick toward a team-mate (a ring shows who) and Pass or Lob within your
  three seconds (the ring over the ball). Shoot from the circle with your GS or GA. In defence, Defend from 0.9 m
  (the ring round the landing foot turns red when you are too close) and Intercept a pass.
- **Team or one bib**: on the home screen, pick Team, or a bib to play one position all match. Does the bib you
  chose feel like that position?
- **The rules**: is every call right? Offside (try Settings > Keep me onside > Off), held ball, obstruction, the
  centre pass alternating, the penalty player standing out of play.
- **Scrolling**: drag any long page (How to Play, Settings, the cup) from anywhere on it, its words included.
- **Move buttons** (Settings, or the pause menu): drag the buttons where your thumb finds them.
- **The Nations Cup**: pools, semi-finals, the bronze match and the final.
- **The Club Season**: does the table, the finals draw and the grand final make sense?
- **A first match** (or Settings > Coach's tips on): did the tips teach you the game within a minute?
- **Your language**: every screen. Note any word that is wrong, cut off or too long.
- **A budget phone**: does it stay smooth on Low? Does the phone get warm? (Settings > Frame rate > Battery saver.)
