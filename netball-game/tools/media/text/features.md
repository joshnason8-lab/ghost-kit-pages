# Features

- **The real rules**: played to World Netball's Rules of Netball (2024 edition, the one in force): footwork and the
  landing foot, three seconds, obstruction at 0.9 m, contact, offside, over a third and the short pass.
- **Two umpires**: they run the side lines, signal every call, award free passes, penalty passes and throw-ins, play
  advantage, and warn, suspend or order off. Choose how often they blow: fewer whistles, standard or the real rate.
- **Seven positions**: GS, GA, WA, C, WD, GD and GK, each kept to her own areas, bibs on; only the Goal Shooter and
  Goal Attack score, from inside the circle.
- **Team or one position**: control whoever has the ball (and the nearest defender), or pick a bib and play one
  position for the whole match.
- **Three modes**: Quick Match with any two of 23 nations, the Nations Cup (pools, semi-finals, bronze and final) and
  the Club Season (eight clubs, seven rounds, semi-finals and a grand final).
- **Three venues**: a floodlit arena, a community sports hall and an outdoor park court under an evening sky; indoors
  on Medium and High, LED boards that cry "GOAL!" and a goal light on each post.
- **AI that plays netball**: attackers drive into the centre third on the whistle, feeders work the circle edge,
  shooters hold near the post, defenders front and cover; four difficulty levels, from Rookie to World class.
- **Broadcast touches**: a team card before the match and at half time, a close shot of the post after a goal, a
  scoreboard, and a box score for every match (goals from attempts, feeds, centre pass receives, gains, intercepts,
  rebounds, penalties).
- **Recorded motion**: the players are a 3D model animated with Mixamo clips, plus real motion capture from Carnegie
  Mellon University's motion capture database for the defensive side-step and the landing after a catch.
- **Crowd and sound**: a crowd that cheers, groans and applauds with the play, recorded referee whistles, catches,
  bounces and footsteps for each surface, and music written for the game.
- **Coach's tips**: a short tip the first time you need each thing in your first match; switch them back on any time.
- **Three graphics levels**: Low, Medium and High, picked for your phone on first run, lowered automatically if a
  match runs slowly, and a battery-saver frame rate.
- **Made for your hands**: buttons in three sizes that you can drag to where your thumbs are, a left-handed layout,
  three cameras, and keyboard controls on devices that have a keyboard.
- **Nineteen languages**: from English, Afrikaans and isiZulu to Chichewa, Kiswahili, Bahasa Melayu, Chinese,
  Sinhala, Tamil, Welsh, Te Reo Māori, Tongan, Samoan and Fijian.
- **Offline and private**: no internet permission, no ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.
- **League options**: the Double Zone (long shots late in each quarter count two), time-outs and rolling changes, and
  quarters of 5, 8, 10 or the full 15 minutes.
