# Netball Unleashed

**Netball by the real rules, on your phone.**

## Short description

Netball Unleashed is a netball game for Android phones, played to World Netball's current Rules of Netball. Take the
centre pass, drive into space, feed the circle and shoot, then win the ball back with an intercept, while two umpires
call footwork, obstruction and offside as they would in a real match. It plays offline, in nineteen languages, with
no ads, no purchases and no account.

## About the game

Netball is the fast passing game played by millions, most of all across the Commonwealth: seven players a side, each
allowed only in her own areas of the court, no running with the ball, three seconds to pass it, and goals scored only
by the Goal Shooter and Goal Attack from inside the goal circle. It rewards movement, timing and quick, accurate
passing. Netball Unleashed is a game built around those rules rather than around a generic ball game.

Every match is played to the Rules of Netball, 2024 edition, the edition in force today. The centre pass alternates
after every goal and must be caught in the centre third; the landing foot, the three seconds, obstruction at 0.9 m,
contact, offside, over a third and the short pass are all called by two umpires who signal each decision. Free
passes, penalty passes, throw-ins and advantage are there, and so are warnings, suspensions and ordering off. Four
quarters with real intervals, and extra time to a two-goal lead when a match must have a winner.

You control the play with a thumb stick and a few buttons: push toward a team-mate and pass or lob within your three
seconds, shoot when your shooter is in the circle, and in defence keep your 0.9 m and leap for the intercept. Play
the whole team, switching to whoever has the ball, or pick a bib and play one position all match. Nothing is played
for you, and a coach's tips in your first match teach the basics within a minute.

Play a Quick Match between any two of 23 netball nations, from Australia, New Zealand, England, Jamaica and South
Africa to Malawi, Uganda, Tonga, Fiji and Sri Lanka; take one through the Nations Cup, with its pools, semi-finals,
bronze match and final; or lead one of eight invented clubs through a Club Season and its finals. Matches are played
in a packed floodlit arena, a community sports hall or an outdoor park court under an evening sky, with a crowd that
reacts to the play.

It is made for the phones people really have: three graphics levels, a battery saver, buttons you can resize and
move, and a left-handed layout. It works with no connection at all and collects no data. Netball Unleashed is an
independent game, not affiliated with or endorsed by any netball governing body, league, club or player; the players
are fictional, the countries and the rules are real.
