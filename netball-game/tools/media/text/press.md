# Press blurb

**Netball Unleashed** is a netball game for Android, played to the sport's real rules. Seven a side, footwork, three
seconds, obstruction at 0.9 m and two umpires who signal every call: take the centre pass, feed the circle, shoot from
inside it and win the ball back with an intercept. Play the whole team or one position, in a Quick Match between 23
nations, the Nations Cup or a Club Season, in a floodlit arena, a sports hall or an outdoor park court. It plays
offline in nineteen languages, with no ads, no purchases and no data collected. Netball Unleashed is made by
independent developer Josh Nason and is coming soon to Google Play.
