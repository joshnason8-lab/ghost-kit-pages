# Netball Unleashed: privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026. Netball Unleashed is a netball game for Android, published by Josh Nason. The same
policy is in the game, under Settings > About > Privacy policy.

## The summary

The game collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no
in-app purchases.

## Internet

The game does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything. Everything it needs
(the licences, this policy and the help) is inside the game.

## What the game keeps on your phone

Your settings (language, graphics, sound, controls, where you moved the buttons, difficulty) and your progress (the
Nations Cup or Club Season you are playing and your record book). They are small files in the game's own private
storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the game deletes
them.

## Permissions

One: vibration, to buzz the phone when your player catches the ball, makes an intercept or your team scores
(Settings turns it off). The game does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

## Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page and the game will say so first, with a new date.
