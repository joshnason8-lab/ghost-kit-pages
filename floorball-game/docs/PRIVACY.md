# Floorball Unleashed: privacy policy
Last updated 27 October 2026. Floorball Unleashed is made by Josh Nason.

## In short
The game collects no personal data. It has no account and no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases, and it does not send anything anywhere.

## The internet
The game does not ask for permission to use the internet (Android's INTERNET permission is off in this build), so it cannot connect to anything: everything it shows, these pages included, is inside the game.

## What stays on your phone
Your settings (language, graphics, sound, controls, where you put the buttons, difficulty), your World Finals and your club season in progress, and your record of results. They are small files in the game's own private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Permissions
One: vibration, to buzz the phone when something happens to you in a match (your goal, a whistle against you). Turn it off in Settings > Vibration. The game does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

## Children
The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes
If this ever changes, this page and the date above will change with the game's next version, before the game does anything new with your information.
