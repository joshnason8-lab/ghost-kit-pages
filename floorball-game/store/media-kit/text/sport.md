# What is floorball?

Floorball is an indoor team sport played with light sticks and a plastic ball full of holes. Each team has five
outfield players and a goalkeeper, who plays mostly on their knees and has no stick. The rink is about 40 by 20 metres,
surrounded by boards about half a metre high with rounded corners, so the ball stays in play and the game rarely
stops. A match has three periods of 20 minutes.

The ball weighs only about 23 grams and has 26 holes, and the sticks are short and light, so the game is fast and
skilful rather than physical. Raising the stick high, playing the ball above the knee, and pushing, tripping or
hooking an opponent's stick are all against the rules, and a referee's two-minute penalty leaves a team a player short.

The sport grew out of plastic stick-and-ball games in Sweden in the late 1960s and 1970s. National federations formed
in the early 1980s, and the international federation was founded in 1986. Men's World Championships have been played
since 1996 and women's since 1997, and floorball is on the programme of the World Games.

It goes by many names: innebandy in Sweden and Norway, salibandy in Finland, unihockey in Switzerland and Germany,
florbal in Czechia and Slovakia. Sweden, Finland, Switzerland and Czechia have long been its strongest nations, and
it is played in schools, clubs and leagues in dozens of countries around the world.
