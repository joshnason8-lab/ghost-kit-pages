# Features

- **The real rules**: the international Rules of the Game in the edition valid from 1 July 2026, with its new rules (no
  field player through the goalkeeper area, a goalkeeper's throw can score, coincidental penalties).
- **Two referees**: they whistle and signal every call (free-hit, advantage, delayed penalty, two minutes, goal), and
  a card on the screen names the offence and its rule.
- **Three ways to play**: Quick match; the World Finals (16 nations: groups, play-offs, quarter-finals, semi-finals
  and medal matches); a club season with ten clubs and 3-2-1-0 points, then semi-finals and a final.
- **22 national teams, men's and women's**: squads of 20 fictional players with names from each country, in kits of
  the country's colours.
- **Three venues**: a city arena with 5,200 seats and a hanging scoreboard, a Nordic sports hall with birch walls, and
  a school gym with wall bars.
- **Team AI that plays like floorball teams**: a 2-1-2 defence, a forecheck, flying line changes, goalkeepers who read
  the shot, and a pulled goalkeeper late in a close match.
- **Motion capture**: players and referees walk, jog and run with real people's recorded movements, their feet
  planted on the floor.
- **A crowd and sound you can hear**: a recorded crowd that rises as an attack builds and cheers a goal, footsteps on
  the hall floor, and real referee whistles.
- **A coach for your first match**: short notes at the bottom of the screen as each moment comes.
- **A box score after every match**: score by period, shots, saves, penalty minutes, power-play goals, face-offs,
  every goal and penalty, the player of the match.
- **Three graphics levels** (Low, Medium, High), chosen for your phone at first start, plus a battery saver and a
  60 fps mode.
- **Plays offline**: no account, no ads, no purchases, no data collected, no internet permission.
- **Fifteen languages**: English, Svenska, Suomi, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Čeština, Slovenčina, Norsk bokmål,
  Dansk, Latviešu, Eesti, Polski, 日本語 and ไทย, with each country's real floorball terms.
- **Controls that fit your hands**: a joystick wherever your thumb lands, three buttons in three sizes that you can
  move anywhere, a left-handed layout, automatic player switching, and a steadier game speed.
