# Floorball Unleashed

**Fast indoor floorball for your phone, played to the real rules.**

## Short description

Floorball Unleashed is a floorball game for Android: five players and a goalkeeper a side, on a 40 by 20 metre rink
inside low boards, with two referees who call the game by the international Rules of the Game. Play a quick match,
take a nation through the World Finals, or run a club through a season. Offline, with no ads, no purchases and no
data collected.

## Long description

Floorball is the fast indoor stick game of Sweden, Finland, Switzerland and Czechia: a light plastic ball with holes in
it, carbon sticks, five outfield players and a goalkeeper on each side, and rinks enclosed by half-metre boards so
the ball almost never stops. It is one of the most played team sports in the Nordic countries, and it is growing
across Europe, Asia and beyond.

Floorball Unleashed puts that game on your phone. You control one outfield player at a time with a joystick and three
buttons (shoot, pass and sprint with the ball; tackle, switch player and sprint without it) while your team-mates play
as floorball teams really play: a 2-1-2 defence, a forecheck, wingers wide, the centre in the slot and line changes
on the fly. Your first match comes with a coach who explains each moment as it happens.

The rules are the real ones, in the edition valid from 1 July 2026: face-offs, hit-ins and free-hits with the
3-metre distance, the goalkeeper area, advantage and delayed penalties, two- and five-minute penalties, power plays,
overtime and penalty shots. Two referees whistle and signal every decision, and a card on the screen tells you which
rule was broken. After the match, a full box score shows every goal, assist, save and penalty.

Play 22 national teams, men's and women's, in a city arena with 5,200 seats, a Nordic sports hall or a school gym.
The players run with real people's motion capture, the hall's crowd cheers and groans with the play, and the referees'
whistles are real recordings. The menus are drawn as a coach's whiteboard.

The game runs offline, in fifteen languages with each country's own floorball words. Three graphics levels let it run
smoothly on budget phones and look its best on fast ones. Floorball Unleashed is an independent game; its players are
fictional, and it is not affiliated with any federation, league or club.
