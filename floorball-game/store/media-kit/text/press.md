# Press blurb

Floorball Unleashed is a floorball game for Android phones from independent developer Josh Nason. Players control one
outfield player at a time in fast five-a-side matches played to the sport's international rules, the 2026 edition,
with two referees who signal every call. It offers a quick match, a 16-nation World Finals and a club season, with 22
men's and women's national teams of fictional players, three venues, motion-captured movement and a recorded crowd.
The game plays offline in fifteen languages and has no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection. Contact:
joshnason8@gmail.com.
