== App name ==
Floorball Unleashed

== Short description ==
Floorball five a side with goalkeepers, to the real rules. Shoot, pass, score!

== Full description ==
Floorball Unleashed is floorball for your phone: the fast indoor stick game of Sweden, Finland, Switzerland and Czechia, played five and a goalkeeper a side on a 40 by 20 metre rink inside the boards.

THE REAL GAME
• Floorball's international Rules of the Game, the edition in force from 1 July 2026: three periods with a stopped clock, face-offs, hit-ins and free-hits with the 3 metre distance, the goal crease and goalkeeper area, two-minute and five-minute penalties, power plays, overtime and penalty shots.
• The new 2026 rules too: no field player through the goalkeeper area, a goalkeeper's throw can score, and coincidental penalties no longer take players off the rink.
• Two referees who whistle and signal every call as real ones do: free-hit, advantage, delayed penalty, two minutes, passive play, goal.
• Late in a match the team behind pulls its goalkeeper for a sixth outfield player; a penalised player walks to the penalty bench and sits out his time.
• Teams that play as floorball is played: a 2-1-2 defence, flying line changes every fifty seconds, wingers wide and the centre in the slot.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Quick match: 22 national teams, men's and women's, in a city arena, a sports hall or a school gym. Period length from 4 to 20 minutes, effective time or a running clock.
• World Finals: sixteen nations in four groups, play-offs, quarter-finals, semi-finals and the medal matches.
• Club season: ten clubs, a league with 3-2-1-0 points (3 for a win in regulation time, 2 after overtime or penalty shots, 1 for an overtime loss), then semi-finals and a final.
• A box score after every match: shots on goal, saves, penalty minutes, power-play goals, every goal and every penalty with its rule.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Fifteen languages: English, Svenska, Suomi, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Čeština, Slovenčina, Norsk, Dansk, Latviešu, Eesti, Polski, 日本語 and ไทย.
• A coach talks you through your first match. A joystick and three buttons, left-handed if you like, in three sizes, and moved wherever your thumbs want them.
• Made to run smoothly on budget and mid-range phones, with a battery saver.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.

Floorball Unleashed is an independent game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any floorball federation, league, club or player. The players are fictional; the countries and the rules are real.
