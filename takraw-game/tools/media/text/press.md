# Sepak Takraw Unleashed: press blurb

Sepak Takraw Unleashed brings sepak takraw, Southeast Asia's acrobatic kick volleyball, to Android phones. Players time
every touch themselves, from the serve with one foot in the circle to the set by the net and the roll spike over the
block, against computer teams modelled on real international matches. It offers Quick Match, a League Season in the
formats of Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, and international championships, across five venues from a national
arena to a beach at sunset. Made by independent developer Josh Nason, it plays offline in thirteen languages, with no
ads, purchases or data collection. Coming soon to Google Play.
