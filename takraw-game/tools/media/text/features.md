# Sepak Takraw Unleashed: features

- **The rules of the sport**: sets to 15 (won by two, up to 17), best of three, the serve changing sides every point, three
  touches a side, faults, line calls and a video challenge.
- **Every event**: regu (three a side), doubles and quadrant, for men and women, and the team event of three regus.
- **Quick Match**: any two teams, any of the five venues.
- **League Season**: a full club season in the formats of Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, with invented clubs.
- **International**: a World Championship, the Royal Cup, the Asian and the Southeast Asian championships.
- **Five venues**: a national arena, a shopping-mall court, a provincial gym, a park court under floodlights and a
  beach at sunset.
- **The moves**: sepak sila, the instep dig, the header, the horse-kick serve, the roll spike, the sunback, the sole
  spike, the feint and the jumping block.
- **Your timing**: nothing is played for you. Move with the joystick, and press as the ring closes on the ball.
- **Help when you want it**: running assist, a slower game speed, bigger buttons and a left-handed layout.
- **Opponents who play like the top sides**: the computer serves, receives and blocks in the patterns of top
  international matches.
- **Motion capture**: the players' kicks, runs and jumps come from motion capture.
- **Living venues**: crowds that follow the ball and cheer, clap and wave the teams' flags; officials in suits;
  substitutes on the benches; recorded sounds of the ball, the crowd and the referee's whistle.
- **Graphics for every phone**: Auto picks Low, Medium or High for your phone, and a battery-saver frame rate is there
  when you want it.
- **Offline and private**: no internet permission, no ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.
- **Thirteen languages**: English, Thai, Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Lao, Burmese, Khmer, Filipino, Japanese,
  Korean, Chinese and Hindi.
