# Sepak Takraw Unleashed

**Sepak takraw, the kick volleyball of Southeast Asia, on your phone.**

## In short

Sepak Takraw Unleashed is a sepak takraw game for Android phones. Serve with one foot in the circle, set the ball high
by the net and finish with a roll spike over the block, in regu, doubles or quadrant, at five venues from a national
arena to a beach at sunset. It plays offline, in thirteen languages, with no ads and no purchases.

## About the game

Sepak takraw is played across Southeast Asia with a light, hollow woven ball. It is like volleyball over a net, but
the ball is played with the feet, the knees, the chest and the head, never the hands, and each side has three
touches to get it back over. Its spikes, where a player flips into the air to kick the ball down over the net, make
it one of the most acrobatic sports there is.

In Sepak Takraw Unleashed nothing is played for you. You run your player to the ball with the joystick and press as
the ring closing on the ball meets it: a pass with the inside of the foot, a set by the net, a roll spike or a
sunback, a jumping block. The computer's teams play the way the top sides do, with hard, flat serves and one blocker
at the net, worked out from real international matches. You choose how much help you get with running, and how fast a
match goes.

Play a Quick Match between any two teams at any venue; a League Season, a full club season in the formats of
Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, with invented clubs; or the International events: a World Championship, the Royal
Cup and the Asian and Southeast Asian championships, with the national teams that really take part.

The players move with motion capture. Officials in suits call the faults and the lines, and a video challenge can
overturn a call. Crowds follow the ball, cheer, clap and wave the two teams' flags, and substitutes watch from the
benches. The game is built to run smoothly on budget phones: it picks Low, Medium or High graphics for yours.
