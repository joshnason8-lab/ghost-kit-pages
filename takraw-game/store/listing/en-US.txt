== App name ==
Sepak Takraw Unleashed

== Short description ==
Sepak takraw: roll spikes, horse-kick serves and blocks at the net. Real rules.

== Full description ==
Sepak Takraw Unleashed is sepak takraw for your phone: the kick volleyball of Southeast Asia. Serve with a foot in the circle, receive with the inside of your foot, set it high by the net, and launch a roll spike over the block.

THE REAL GAME
• The laws of the game as they stand today: sets to 15 (win by two, up to 17), best of three, the serve alternating every point, three touches a side.
• Regu, quadrant, doubles and the team event, for men and women, on a court and net to the exact measurements.
• The moves fans know: sepak sila, the instep dig, the header, the horse-kick serve, the roll spike, the sunback, the sole spike, the feint and the block.
• Faults called by the referee, line judges' calls, and a video challenge to overturn them.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Quick Match: any two teams, any venue.
• League Season: a full club season in the formats of Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, with invented clubs. Malaysia: seven Grand Prix, points for every finish, promotion and relegation. Thailand: home and away, every fixture a tie of two regus. Indonesia: five regions, then the national finals in Jakarta.
• International: a World Championship, the Royal Cup, the Asian and the Southeast Asian championships, with Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia, Myanmar, Japan, Korea and more.

FIVE VENUES
The national arena, a shopping-mall court, a provincial gym, a park court under the lights and a beach at sunset.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Thirteen languages: English, ไทย, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ລາວ, မြန်မာ, ខ្មែរ, Filipino, 日本語, 한국어, 中文 and हिन्दी.
• Nothing is played for you: run your player to the ball with the joystick and kick as the ring closes on it. Choose how much the game helps you run, and how fast the match goes.
• Made to run smoothly on budget and mid-range phones.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.

Sepak Takraw Unleashed is an independent game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any sepak takraw federation, league, club or player. The clubs' nicknames are invented and the players are fictional; the countries, places and rules are real.
