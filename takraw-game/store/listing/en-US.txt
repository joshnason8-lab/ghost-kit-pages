== App name ==
Takraw Smash

== Short description ==
Sepak takraw: roll spikes, horse-kick serves and blocks at the net. ISTAF rules.

== Full description ==
Takraw Smash is sepak takraw for your phone: the kick volleyball of Southeast Asia. Serve with a foot in the circle, receive with the inside of your foot, set it high by the net, and launch a roll spike over the block.

THE REAL GAME
• The ISTAF laws as they stand: sets to 15 (win by two, up to 17), best of three, the serve alternating every point, three touches a side.
• Regu, quadrant, doubles and the team event, for men and women, on a court and net to the exact measurements.
• The moves fans know: sepak sila, the instep dig, the header, the horse-kick serve, the roll spike, the sunback, the sole spike, the feint and the block.
• Faults called by the referee, line judges' calls, and a video challenge to overturn them.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Quick Match: any two teams, any venue.
• League Season: the real leagues, run the real way. Malaysia's Sepak Takraw League: seven Grand Prix, points for every finish, promotion and relegation. The Takraw Thailand League: home and away, every fixture a tie of two regus. Liga Sepak Takraw Indonesia: five regions, then the national finals in Jakarta.
• International: the World Cup, the Royal Cup, the Asian and the Southeast Asian championships, with Thailand, Malaysia, Vietnam, Indonesia, Myanmar, Japan, Korea and more.

EIGHT VENUES
The national arena, a grand dome, a shopping-mall court, a provincial gym, a temple court, a park court under the lights, a beach at sunset and a night-market street.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Thirteen languages: English, ไทย, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Tiếng Việt, ລາວ, မြန်မာ, ខ្មែរ, Filipino, 日本語, 한국어, 中文 and हिन्दी.
• Nothing is played for you: run your player to the ball with the joystick and kick as the ring closes on it. Choose how much the game helps you run, and how fast the match goes.
• Made to run smoothly on budget phones like the Galaxy A16.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.
