# Sepak takraw: what the game is built on

Researched in October 2026 from the ISTAF Laws of the Game (the 2013, 2016 and 2024 editions, as quoted by national
federations), ISTAF's letter of 22 December 2023, and news reports. **[UNCERTAIN]** marks what sources disagree on or
could not confirm; the game's choice is given beside it.

## The rules the game plays

| Rule | Real game | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| Players | Regu 3 (tekong, feeder, striker), Quadrant 4, Doubles 2; Team Regu is three regus, best of three matches | All four events |
| Court | 13.40 × 6.10 m (a badminton doubles court), lines 4 cm and part of the court, centre line 2 cm | Exact |
| Quarter circles | Radius 0.90 m, centred where the centre line meets each side line, at the net | Exact |
| Service circle | Radius 0.30 m, centre 2.45 m from the back line, on the court's middle (3.05 m from each side line) | Exact |
| Net | 1.52 m men, 1.42 m women at the centre (1.55 / 1.45 at the posts); 0.70 m deep, mesh 6-8 cm, tape top and bottom | Exact |
| Posts | 0.30 m outside the side lines | Exact |
| Ball | Synthetic, 12 holes, 20 crossings; men 170-180 g, women 150-160 g | 175 g / 155 g |
| Scoring | **Since 1 February 2024**: rally point, sets to 15, from 14-all win by 2 up to 17; best of three sets | Default; 21-point sets (the 2013-2024 rule, deuce to 25) as an option |
| Service | **Since 1 February 2024**: alternates every point, whoever wins it (it was three serves a side, 2013-2024) | Alternates every point |
| The serve | The score is called; an inside player throws the ball to the tekong, who kicks it over the net with his non-kicking foot in the service circle. Both of the serving side's inside players stand in their quarter circles; the receivers anywhere on their side | Exact, with the faults below |
| Ends | Change after each set, and in the third set when a side reaches 8 | Exact |
| Time-outs | One a side a set, up to a minute | One a side a set |
| Touches | At most three a side; one player may make all three | Exact |
| Block | **[UNCERTAIN]** whether a block counts as one of the three | Counts as one |
| Challenge | Video review with high-speed cameras: trialled at the 2022 King's Cup, compulsory since the 2025 SEA Games; **[UNCERTAIN]** how many | One a set, kept if right |

### Faults the game calls
- Serving: the tekong lifts his standing foot or jumps; an inside player out of his quarter circle; the ball touching
  the serving side before it crosses; the serve not crossing between the boundary tapes, or landing out.
- In play: more than three touches; the ball on a hand or arm; a player touching the net; a foot over the net or the
  centre line under it before the ball is played; the ball under the net or outside the boundary tapes; the ball out.

### Officials
Referee and assistant referee on either side of the net, four line judges on the side lines and two on the back lines
(smaller events: two at the corners).

## Positions and moves
- **Tekong**: the server, at the back. **Feeder** (Malay *apit kiri*, Thai *tua chong*): sets. **Striker** (*apit
  kanan*, Thai *tua faat*): spikes. Roles are fixed; there is no rotation.
- Kicks: *sepak sila* (inside of the foot, the basic receive and set), *sepak kuda* (instep, for digs), toe poke,
  header (*tandukan*), chest, thigh and shoulder controls.
- Serves: the instep serve, and the **horse-kick (sunback) serve**, kicked with the sole over the shoulder.
- Spikes: the **roll spike** (a full somersault, the Thai *gulung*), the **sunback** (a jumping horse kick, legs
  scissoring: the Malaysian *kalih*), the **sole spike** (*tapak*), the **header spike**, and the soft feint.
- Block: a jump at the net with the back or side to it.

## Competitions (formats the game follows, with fictional names)
- **ISTAF World Cup**: about 20 nations, groups then semi-finals and a final; both losing semi-finalists take bronze.
  Recent: 2025 Patna (India won men's regu), 2026 Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia awarded the team title after Thailand walked
  off over a review).
- **King's Cup** (the ISTAF World Championship, Thailand, yearly), often played in a shopping mall's hall (Fashion Island,
  Bangkok; Central Hatyai).
- **Asian Games** (since 1990, every four years; Aichi-Nagoya 2026 had eight events, Japan's first gold in quadrant),
  **SEA Games** (every two years, with hoop takraw and chinlone too), **ASTAF Asian Cup** (from 2025).
- Strongest men: Thailand, Malaysia, Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia, Myanmar, Korea, India, the Philippines, Laos.
  Women: Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Myanmar, Laos, the Philippines, India, Japan, China, Korea.

## Club leagues
- **Malaysia, Sepak Takraw League** (since 2014): 12 Premier clubs named "state + English mascot", a Grand Prix series
  with a split into Class One and Class Two, relegation; Thai imports allowed.
- **Thailand, Takraw Thailand League** (since 2002): clubs named "epithet + province"; round robin.
- **Indonesia**: a national league from 2026 across five zones.
- The game: three fictional leagues in those styles, a round robin then a Final Four (semi-finals and a final).

## Venues
The elite game is played **indoors** on a synthetic mat: Titiwangsa Stadium ("Home of Sepak Takraw", about 3,000),
Juara Stadium (1,000-2,500), and shopping-mall halls for the King's Cup. Grassroots takraw is outdoors: school and
temple courts, park courts in the evening, beach takraw (Asian Beach Games). The game has four indoor and four outdoor
venues.

## Fans
Thailand against Malaysia is the great rivalry; Vietnam's women are the rising force. Crowds are loud ("Malaysia
boleh!"), drums and clappers are common at Southeast Asian indoor sport, and broadcasts replay spikes in slow motion.
The traditional games: *sepak raga bulatan* (a circle keeping the ball up), hoop takraw (*takraw lod huang*, a
three-ring basket hung high), Myanmar's chinlone.

## Phones
Samsung leads Thailand and Vietnam, Xiaomi Malaysia and Indonesia, Transsion the Philippines. The Galaxy A16 5G was the
best-selling Android phone in Q2 2025: Mali-G57 MC2 (Dimensity 6300 or Helio G99) or Mali-G68 MP2 (Exynos 1330),
4 GB RAM. The game's Low graphics are set for that class of phone.
