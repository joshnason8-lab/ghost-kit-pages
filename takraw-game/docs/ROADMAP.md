# Roadmap

## Next

- **Meshy players and referees** (docs/MESHY_BRIEF.md): order the eight models, then fit them to the moves.
- **A phone test on a Galaxy A16** (or any Mali-G57 phone): frame times on Low and Medium in the Grand Dome, the
  biggest venue; trim the crowd or the render scale if it drops below 60.
- **Native speakers' review** of the translations' sport terms: the horse-kick serve, the sunback and the sole spike
  have no settled names in some languages (the translators' notes are in the pull request).
- **The release key and the closed test** (store/PLAY_STORE.md).
- **Real club names**, if the leagues agree to it (the clubs are their real states and services under invented
  nicknames for now), and the **TTL's 2027 format** (16 clubs, ten circuits) once its details are out.

## Ideas fans would enjoy

- **Hoop takraw** (*takraw lod huang*): a timed mode kicking through the three-ring basket for style points, as at
  the SEA Games.
- **Sepak raga bulatan**: keep the ball up in a circle with team-mates, as long as you can.
- **Create a player** and a career from a provincial club to the national team.
- **The 3-serve rule of 2013-2024** as an option, for the old-school.
- **Commentary** in Thai and Malay, and crowd chants ("Malaysia boleh!").
- **Mixed quadrant**, new at the 2026 Asian Games.
