Floorstorm

Floorball for Android: five and a goalkeeper a side on a 40 by 20 metre rink, to the international Rules of the Game in force from 1 July 2026. Twenty-two national teams, men's and women's, a World Finals tournament, two referees who signal every call, in fifteen languages.

Floorstorm is an independent game, not affiliated with or endorsed by any floorball federation, league, club or player. The players are fictional.
