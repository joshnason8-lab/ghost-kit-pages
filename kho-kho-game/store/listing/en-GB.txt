== App name ==
Kho Kho Rush

== Short description ==
Kho kho to the real rules: sit in the lane, give the kho, chase and pole dive.

== Full description ==
Kho Kho Rush is kho kho for your phone: the chasing game of India. Sit in the lane with your side, rise on a kho, chase the defenders to the pole, and dive to touch them out before the turn runs down.

THE REAL GAME
• Two rule books. Classic, as the national championships play it: nine a side, eight seated in the squares, a 27 m field, 1 point an out, and the follow-on. Fast, as the 2025 world championship played it: seven a side, a 22 m field, 2 points an out, dream runs, the wazir and the power play.
• Every rule of the active chaser: take your direction and keep to it, never cross the central lane, turn only round a pole, and give the kho from behind before you pass the cross lane.
• Defenders on in batches of three. Out by a touch, a sky dive, a pole dive, a step over the line or a late entry.
• Umpires on both side lines who whistle a foul until it is put right, a referee and a timekeeper. Extra innings and the minimum chase settle a tied knockout.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Quick Match: any two sides, any ground, either rule book. You call the toss and choose to chase or defend.
• Championships: sixteen nations at the world championship, or sixteen states at the national championship: groups, quarter-finals, semi-finals, the final. Men's and women's.
• City League: eight invented city sides play each other home and away under the fast rules, then the top four go to the semi-finals.
• Watch a Match: two sides play it out on their own.

FOUR GROUNDS
A village ground of beaten earth at dusk, a school ground in the morning, an indoor arena on mats, and a floodlit stadium of red earth.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Fourteen languages: English, हिन्दी, मराठी, ಕನ್ನಡ, తెలుగు, தமிழ், മലയാളം, ଓଡ଼ିଆ, বাংলা, ગુજરાતી, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, অসমীয়া, नेपाली and සිංහල.
• Coach tips walk you through your first match, chasing and defending.
• Simple touch controls: a joystick for your chaser, KHO and DIVE. Move the buttons where your thumb finds them, make them bigger, or play left-handed.
• Turns of 3 or 5 minutes for a quick game, or the real length.
• Made to run smoothly on budget and mid-range phones.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.

Kho Kho Rush is an independent game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any kho kho federation, league, club or player. The players are fictional; the countries, states and rules are real.
