# Kho Kho Rush

**Sit. Kho. Chase.**

## Short description

Kho Kho Rush is the chasing game of India on your phone. Sit in the lane with your side, rise on a "kho", run the
defenders down to the pole and dive to touch them out before the turn runs out. It plays offline, in fourteen
languages, to the sport's own rules.

## About the game

Kho kho is one of South Asia's oldest team games: a game of tag where one side kneels in a line down the middle of
the field, facing alternate ways, and only one of them may chase at a time. To pass the chase on, the runner touches
a seated team-mate on the back and calls "kho!", and that player springs up and takes over. The defenders, three at a
time, dodge and weave round the line and the poles at each end for as long as they can.

Kho Kho Rush plays it the way it is played. Every rule of the active chaser is there: he picks a direction and must
keep to it, may not cross the central lane, turns only round a pole, and gives the kho from behind. Umpires on both
side lines whistle fouls until they are put right. You can play the classic game, nine a side on a 27 m field, or
the faster seven-a-side game with its dream runs, wazir and power play.

You can chase and defend yourself, or watch two sides play it out. The computer's players were tuned against real
matches: defenders who are out of danger stand and watch, the one under threat plays tight round the pole, and
chasers pass the kho, swing round the pole and dive at the last moment. Their moves come from motion capture.

Play a quick match on four grounds, from a village field at dusk to a floodlit stadium, or take a side through a
national or world championship or a city league. The crowd follows the chase and roars when a dive just misses.

The game needs no account and no internet, collects no data and has no ads or purchases. It runs on older phones
too, with three graphics levels and a battery saver.
