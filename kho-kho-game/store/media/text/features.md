# Features

- **The real rules**: the active chaser's direction, the central lane, turning only round a pole, the kho from
  behind, outs, fouls, turns, the follow-on and extra innings, as the rule books have them.
- **Two ways to play**: the classic game (nine a side, 27 m field, 1 point an out) and the fast game (seven a side,
  22 m field, 2 points an out, dream runs, the wazir and the power play).
- **Game modes**: Quick Match; Championships (a world championship of sixteen nations, a national championship of
  sixteen states, a city league of eight invented city sides); Watch a Match.
- **Four grounds**: a village ground at dusk, a school ground in the morning, an indoor arena on mats, and a
  floodlit stadium at night, each dressed as for a tournament.
- **Chase or defend**: one thumb runs, buttons give the kho, dive, dodge and switch defender.
- **Real moves**: the kho, the sky dive, the pole dive and the pole turn, where the chaser swings round the pole
  and comes out running.
- **AI tuned against real matches**: four difficulty levels, from Beginner to International.
- **Motion capture**: the players move with recorded motion (Mixamo animations and the CMU motion capture
  database).
- **A crowd that watches**: spectators turn to follow the chase, and the roar builds at the big moments.
- **Real sounds**: referee whistles, the slap of the kho, footsteps on earth and mats, crowds and the evening
  outdoors.
- **Fourteen languages**: English and thirteen languages of South Asia.
- **Plays offline**: no account, no internet, no ads, no purchases, no data collected.
- **Runs on older phones**: Low, Medium and High graphics, chosen for the phone and lowered by itself if a match
  runs slowly; a battery saver frame rate.
- **Comfort and access**: left-handed layout, three button sizes, buttons you can move, lane assist, coach tips
  for a first match, and shirt numbers over the players.
