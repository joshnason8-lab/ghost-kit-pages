# Press blurb

Kho Kho Rush brings kho kho, India's traditional chasing game, to Android phones. Players kneel in the lane, pass
the chase with a touch and a shout of "kho!", and run down defenders round the poles, to the sport's real rules in
both its classic nine-a-side and fast seven-a-side forms. The game offers quick matches, world and national
championships and a city league across four grounds, from a village field at dusk to a floodlit stadium. It plays
offline in fourteen languages, collects no data, has no ads, and is made to run well on older phones.
