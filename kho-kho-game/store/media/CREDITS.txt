Kho Kho Rush - media kit credits

Everything in this kit was captured from the game itself (version VERSION), made by Josh Nason with the
Godot Engine (godotengine.org, MIT licence). Every team's players are invented. No real player, club, league,
competition or federation is named or shown.

You may use these pictures, films and texts to write about or show Kho Kho Rush.

In the pictures and films
- The players: the maker's own athlete model, moved by animations from Mixamo (Adobe) and the CMU Graphics Lab
  Motion Capture Database (mocap.cs.cmu.edu; created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217).
- The grounds: Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0: skies, surfaces, chairs, benches, lights and trees. The maker's own
  models, made with Meshy (meshy.ai): rain trees, palms, grass, scorers' table, buildings and temple. Everything
  else made in code for the game.
- Lettering: Baloo 2 (Copyright 2019 The Baloo 2 Project Authors) and Noto Sans (Copyright The Noto Project
  Authors), SIL Open Font License 1.1.

In the trailers' sound
- Music: written for the game (Kho Kho Rush's menu music).
- The crowd: "Free Crowd Cheering Sounds", by Gregor Quendel (opengameart.org), CC-BY 4.0.
- The kho's slap: "Punch, slap, n' kick", by CGEffex (freesound 98341), cut by congusbongus, CC-BY 3.0.
- Whistles: Pixabay (pixabay.com), Pixabay Content License: charlesheffley009, framptones, freesound_community.
- Footsteps and landings: Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0. Crickets, birds and applause: OpenGameArt, CC0.
- Some crowd reactions and players' voices: from Kabaddi Raid, by the same maker.
