Help

Playing

  • Your player has the gold ring. Your left thumb anywhere on the left of the screen runs him; the buttons on the right are what he can do now. SWITCH changes him to the teammate best placed for the ball.
  • With the sliotar: STRIKE shoots for a point (or, with the joystick pushed another way, strikes a long ball to the teammate there); GOAL, close in, drives it low for the net; PASS hand-passes it; SOLO puts it on your hurley before your four steps are up (the dots over your player count them), and HAND takes it back, your second and last catch.
  • Without it: CATCH or LIFT it when it comes near, PULL to strike it first time; near the man on the ball, TACKLE, and BLOCK when he goes to strike.
  • Restarts: your puck-out LONG or SHORT; your free, 65 or sideline cut STRIKE at the posts or PASS; a penalty, aim and GOAL.
  • Too much to manage? Settings > Help with the ball > Full: your player solos by himself and runs for loose balls when your thumb is off the joystick.

The rules

The game plays the playing rules of hurling in force in 2026: two halves of 35 minutes (30 at club level), a goal worth three points, four steps and two catches, the 13-metre and 20-metre rules at restarts, yellow and red cards, the black card and sin bin for denying a goal chance, extra time and penalties in knock-out games. How to Play in the game has the whole of it.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Phones with less memory start on Low by themselves.
  • The beta will not install on an older or Android Go phone: use the file ending in -32bit.apk from the beta page.
  • The Irish looks wrong (an bhfuil an Ghaeilge mícheart?): please tell us which screen and what it says; we want it right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size, Move buttons to drag them where you want them, and Left-handed to swap the sides.
