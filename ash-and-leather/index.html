Hurling Heroes

Hurling for Android, to the playing rules as they stand: fifteen a side, goals and points, the four steps and two catches, puck-outs, frees, 65s, sideline cuts and penalties, the referee, his linesmen and the umpires with their white and green flags. Twenty counties, a parish field at dusk, a county ground and the national stadium, in English and in Irish (ar fáil as Gaeilge).

Hurling Heroes is an independent game, not connected with or endorsed by any sporting body, county board or club. The counties appear by their place names, in colours of the game's own design; every player is invented.
