Hurling Heroes · Hurling for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

Hurling Heroes is a hurling game for Android phones, published by Josh Nason. This policy explains what the game does with your information. It is short, because the game does very little with it.

The summary

Hurling Heroes collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The game uses the internet for one thing only, and only if you ask it to: downloading the optional HD pack for High graphics (below). It sends nothing about you.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your settings: language, graphics, sound, controls (including where you moved the buttons) and difficulty.
  • Your progress: the championship you are playing, a match you left part-way, and your record book.

These are small files in the game's own private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

The HD pack

High graphics can use an extra download, the HD pack. Only if you choose to download it, the game fetches it from GitHub (github.com), where the game's files are published. As with any download, GitHub's servers see your phone's internet address; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that. The game sends no information about you, your phone or your play, and nothing else ever uses the internet.

Permissions

The game asks for two permissions: vibration, to buzz the phone when something happens to your player (you can turn it off in Settings), and internet, used only to download the HD pack when you ask for it. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

In the game

This policy and the help page are also in the game itself, in Settings > About; the game opens no web pages.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does, and the date at the top will change.
