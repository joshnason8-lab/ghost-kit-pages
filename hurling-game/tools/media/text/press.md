# Press blurb

Hurling Heroes is a new Android game of hurling, the fast and ancient Irish field sport, played fifteen a side to
the real 2026 rules. Players control one hurler at a time with simple touch controls, lifting, soloing and striking
the sliotar for points and goals, while the referee, linesmen and umpires officiate as in the real game. Matches are
played at a parish field, a county ground under floodlights and a packed national stadium, singly or through a full
summer championship. The game runs on everyday phones, works offline, has no ads or purchases, and is available in
English and Irish.
