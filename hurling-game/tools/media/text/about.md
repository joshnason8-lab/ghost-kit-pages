# Hurling Heroes

**Hurling, as it is played.**

## Short description

Hurling Heroes brings Ireland's ancient field sport to your phone: fifteen a side, the clash of the ash, goals and
points, played to the real rules. Pick a county and a ground for a quick match, or take one through the Summer
Championship to the final, in English or Irish.

## About the game

Hurling is one of the oldest field games in the world and one of the fastest: two teams of fifteen, a stick of ash
called a hurley, and a small leather ball, the sliotar, struck the length of the field or carried on the hurley at a
run. A goal under the crossbar is worth three points; a point over it, between the posts, one.

Hurling Heroes plays the game as it is played now, to the 2026 rules: four steps with the sliotar in hand before it
must be bounced or soloed on the hurley, the puck-outs, frees, 65s, sideline cuts and penalties, the shoulder and the
hook and the block, and the yellow, red and black cards. The referee, linesmen and umpires do their jobs on the field,
with the whistle, the flags and the signals of the real game.

You play the man with the gold ring: a thumbstick on the left of the screen runs him, and the buttons on the right are
what he can do at that moment: strike for a point, drive for goal, hand-pass, catch a dropping ball, pull first time,
tackle. Run onto a loose ball and he lifts it and solos away himself. The rest of your side plays around you, and the
other side presses and covers like a championship team.

There are four grounds to play at: a parish field at dusk, a county ground by day and under floodlights, and a full
national stadium, each with its crowd, its sound and its weather of sky. Matches run from eight minutes to the full
seventy. Play a single match, take your county through the Summer Championship (with the Tier Two Cup for the rest),
or sit back and watch two counties play.

It is made to play well on the phones people actually have: three graphics levels, picked for your phone the first
time it runs, with an optional HD download for the sharpest look on recent high-end phones. It plays offline, with no
ads, no purchases, no account and no data collected, in English and in Irish.

Hurling Heroes is an independent game. It is not connected with or endorsed by any sporting body, county board or
club. The counties appear by their place names, in colours of the game's own design; every player is invented.
