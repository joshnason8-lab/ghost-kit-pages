# Features

- **The real rules**: the 2026 playing rules: four steps, the solo, two catches, puck-outs, frees, 65s, sideline cuts, penalties, advantage and added time.
- **Three ways to play**: Throw-in for a quick match, the Summer Championship (groups, then knock-out to the final, with the Tier Two Cup), and Watch a match.
- **A championship your way**: simulate any match you would rather not play, finish one early with a simulated result, or leave one and pick it up later where you left it.
- **Four grounds**: a parish field at dusk, a county ground by day and under floodlights, and a national stadium with its stands full.
- **Officials who do their job**: the referee's whistle, frees and cards (yellow, red and black); linesmen's flags; umpires' white and green flags and their signals for a wide and a 65.
- **Team-mates and opponents who play the game**: sides that hold their shape, press, cover and swarm the ball, tuned to the numbers of real championship hurling.
- **Simple touch controls**: a thumbstick and buttons for what your player can do right now; bigger buttons, buttons you can move, and a left-handed layout.
- **Help with the ball if you want it**: run onto a loose ball and your player lifts it and solos himself; a coach talks you through your first match.
- **Motion capture**: players walk, jog and sprint with recorded human movement, and swing, catch and strike with motion-captured moves.
- **Crowd and sound**: a stadium crowd recorded live that builds as a score comes and roars for a goal, the referee's whistle, hurleys clashing and birdsong round the smaller grounds.
- **Three graphics levels**: Low, Medium and High, picked for your phone and changed any time; High adds real lighting and shadows.
- **The HD pack**: an optional download for High on recent high-end phones, with full-detail players, 4K grass and every swing at 60 frames a second.
- **English and Irish**: the whole game in either language, with the game's own Irish terms.
- **Plays offline**: no internet needed to play; no ads, no purchases, no account, no data collected.
- **Match lengths for any moment**: from 8 minutes to the full 70.
