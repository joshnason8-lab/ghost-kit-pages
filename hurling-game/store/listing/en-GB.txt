== App name ==
Ash & Leather

== Short description ==
Hurling: fifteen a side, the real rules, every county. As Gaeilge too.

== Full description ==
Ash & Leather is hurling for your phone: the clash of the ash, fifteen a side, goals and points, on a parish field at dusk or in front of a full stadium. Lift the sliotar, solo through, and strike it over the bar.

Ar fáil as Gaeilge / Available in Irish.

THE REAL GAME
• The playing rules as they stand in 2026: two halves of 35 minutes (or 30 at club level), a goal worth three points, the score written goals-points.
• Four steps with the sliotar in your hand, then solo it on your hurley; catch it twice at most before you play it away.
• Puck-outs from the small rectangle, frees, 65s, sideline cuts and penalties, each from where the rules put it, with everyone 13 metres back.
• The tackle: the shoulder, the hook, the block and the flick, and the fouls a referee gives frees for. Yellow, red and, in senior county hurling, the black card and the sin bin for denying a goal chance.
• Advantage, added time, and in a knock-out game extra time and a penalty shoot-out.

THE OFFICIALS
The referee whistles, points the free and holds up the card; the linesmen flag the sideline cuts; the umpires in their white coats wave the white flag for a point and the green for a goal, cross their arms for a wide and raise an arm for a 65.

WAYS TO PLAY
• Throw-in: pick two counties and a ground, and play.
• The Summer Championship: two groups of five, then group finals, quarter-finals, semi-finals and the final. The other ten counties play the Tier Two Cup.
• Watch a match: two counties played by the computer, from the television gantry.

THE GROUNDS
A parish field at dusk with its ball-stop nets and floodlights, a county ground by day and under lights, and the national stadium.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• English or Irish, with the game's own Irish terms: camán, sliotar, cúl, cúilín, poc amach.
• A joystick on the left, the buttons for what your player can do on the right: make them bigger, move them where your thumb wants them, or swap the sides if you are left-handed.
• Choose how long a match takes (8 to 70 minutes), how hard the other side plays, and how much help you get with the ball.
• Made to run smoothly on budget and mid-range phones.
• Plays offline. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no data collected.

Ash & Leather is an independent game. It is not connected with or endorsed by any sporting body, county board or club. The counties appear by their place names, in colours of the game's own design; every player is invented.
