Floorball Unleashed · Floorball for Android

Help

Playing

  • You play one outfield player, the one with the yellow number; your team-mates and both goalkeepers play themselves. Move with the joystick: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left of the screen.
  • With the ball: Shoot (push the joystick up or down as you press to pick a corner), Pass (to the team-mate the joystick points at), and hold Sprint to run flat out.
  • Without it: Tackle plays your stick at the ball. From the front it often wins it; from behind or on the stick it is a free-hit to them, and a trip or a slash is two minutes. Switch takes over the team-mate nearest the ball.
  • Free-hits and hit-ins: when your player takes one, press Pass or Shoot. You may score straight from either.
  • Too fast? Settings > Game speed > Steady (the default) plays a little slower than real floorball.

The rules

The game plays floorball's international Rules of the Game, the edition in force from 1 July 2026: three periods, five and a goalkeeper a side, free-hits with the 3 metre distance, two-minute penalties that end when the other team scores on the power play, overtime and penalty shots. How to Play in the game has all of it, with the rule numbers and the referees' signs.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Budget phones start on Low by themselves, and the game lowers the graphics itself if a match runs slowly.
  • Words in your language look wrong: please tell us which screen and what it says; the translations are new and we want them right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size, Left-handed to swap the sides, and Move buttons to put each one where your thumb wants it.
