POK TA POK RIVALS: MEDIA KIT CREDITS

Every screenshot and every second of the trailers is the game itself, rendered and recorded by the game (Godot
Engine's Movie Maker) on High graphics. Nothing is painted over it except the title and end cards and the captions,
which are set in the game's own fonts. The game is made by Josh Nason.

THE PICTURES
- The game, its code, the courts, the ball, the players' gear, the stands and the menus: made for the game.
- The players, the judge, the townspeople in the stands and the carved stone ring: made with Meshy for this game.
  Owned by the game's maker.
- The run and the serve's toss: motion capture from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database
  (mocap.cs.cmu.edu), created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217. Free for research and commercial use.
- The surfaces (dry ground, brown mud, plastered wall, white sandstone blocks, broken wall, sparse grass), the skies
  (Qwantani Dusk 2, Qwantani Noon, Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy, Kloppenheim 02), the island trees and the boulder:
  Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0.
- Fonts: Oswald (Copyright 2016 The Oswald Project Authors) and Noto Sans (Copyright 2022 The Noto Project
  Authors), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
