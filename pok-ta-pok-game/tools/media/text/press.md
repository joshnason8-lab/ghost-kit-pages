# Press blurb

Pok Ta Pok Rivals brings the Mesoamerican ballgame, more than three thousand years old, to Android phones. Players
strike a heavy rubber ball with the hip alone, as ulama is still played in the villages of Sinaloa, Mexico, with its
real swinging count to eight. A reconstructed classic stone court adds sloping walls and a ring that wins the game
outright. Single matches, an eight-town tournament and a watch mode run across four courts, with sixteen towns,
realistic players drawn from motion capture, and six languages. The game is made by independent developer Josh
Nason. It is fully offline, with no ads, no purchases and no data collected. Contact: joshnason8@gmail.com.
