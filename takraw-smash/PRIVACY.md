# Sepak Takraw Unleashed: Privacy Policy

*Last updated 26 October 2026.*

Sepak Takraw Unleashed is a sepak takraw game for Android phones, published by Josh Nason. This policy explains what
the game does with your information. It is short, because the game does very little with it. (The same policy is in
the game, under Settings > About > Privacy policy, and in `privacy.html` next to this file.)

## The summary

Sepak Takraw Unleashed collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no
advertising and no in-app purchases. The game does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send
anything anywhere.

## What the game keeps on your phone

- Your settings: language, graphics, sound, controls and difficulty.
- Your progress: the league season or tournament you are playing, and your record book.

These are small files in the game's own private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never sent
anywhere, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, to buzz the phone when your player touches the ball (you can turn it off
in Settings). It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

## Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

## In the game

Settings > About shows this policy and the help in the game itself, in your language: they need no internet. The
help's "Email us" button opens your phone's email app, and what that app does is covered by its own privacy policy.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does, and the date at the top will change.
