Help

Playing

  • Timing is everything. When the ball is yours, a ring closes on it. Press as it meets the gold circle: PERFECT is the best touch.
  • Serving: Serve for the steady instep serve, Horse kick for the fast sole serve. Aim with the joystick.
  • Setting and spiking: your feeder sets (high for a roll spike, quick for a sunback); your striker spikes. Push the joystick to aim; Feint drops it over the block.
  • Blocking: when they set their striker, Block appears. Jump as he hits.
  • Start with Training on the home screen: a match with a hint at every touch.

The rules

The game plays the International Sepak Takraw Federation's laws as they stand: sets to 15 (from 14-all, win by two, up to 17), best of three, the serve alternating every point, three touches a side. How to Play in the game has the whole of it.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Phones like the Galaxy A16 start on Low by themselves.
  • Words in your language look wrong: please tell us which screen and what it says; the translations are new and we want them right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size, and Left-handed to swap the sides.
