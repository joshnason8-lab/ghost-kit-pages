Pok Ta Pok Rivals

The hip ballgame of Mexico for Android. Play ulama de cadera as it is still played in the villages of southern Sinaloa: a heavy rubber ball struck with the hip across the analco of a long earth court, and the swinging count of rayas to eight, urria and all. Or play the classic stone court, reconstructed from the old chronicles: an I-shaped court with sloped walls, and a stone ring that wins the game outright, very rarely.

Sixteen towns from Sinaloa, the Yucatán, Quintana Roo, Chiapas, Guatemala, Belize and Honduras, a town tournament, four courts from a village at dusk to a stone court by torchlight, in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Italian (también en español).

Pok Ta Pok Rivals is an independent game, not connected with or endorsed by any sporting body, federation, tournament or club. Towns appear by their place names only, with nicknames and colours of the game's own design; every player is invented.
