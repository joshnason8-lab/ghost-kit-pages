Pok Ta Pok Rivals · The hip ballgame for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Pok Ta Pok Rivals is a game of the Mesoamerican hip ballgame for Android phones, published by Josh Nason. This policy explains what the game does with your information. It is short, because the game does very little with it.

The summary

Pok Ta Pok Rivals collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases. The game does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your settings: language, graphics, sound, controls (including where you moved the buttons), the rules you chose and difficulty.
  • Your progress: the town tournament you are playing.

These are small files in the game's own private storage on your phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, to buzz the phone when something happens to your player (you can turn it off in Settings). It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

Links

Settings > About opens this page and the help page in your phone's browser. What the browser does is covered by its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the game does, and the date at the top will change.
