Help

Playing

  • Your player has the gold ring. Your left thumb anywhere on the left of the screen moves him; the game hands you the one of your side who goes for the ball.
  • HIP strikes the ball with your hip: tap it just before the ball reaches you (the swing takes a quarter of a second). The ball's shadow shows how high it is; a pale ring on the earth marks where it comes down to hip height in your half.
  • DIVE takes you down to the ground to meet a low or rolling ball with your hip.
  • Aiming: hold the joystick as you strike. Toward their end, a long high ball; back toward yours, a low hard one that skids along the earth; across, to that side.
  • SERVE when your side serves: rolled along the earth when the count is level, thrown high when a side holds it.
  • Too hard? Settings > Help > On: your player goes to meet the ball when your thumb is off the joystick, and the timing is yours.

The rules

Ulama de cadera: the hip only; a raya goes to the other side when the ball dies in your half, goes past your end line, goes out at the side from your strike, or touches one of you anywhere but the hip. One side holds the count at a time: a raya to it adds one, a raya to the other side takes one off, and in urria a raya against it wipes out its whole count. Eight wins. The classic court is a reconstruction: points to eight, the ball plays off the sloped walls, and the ball through a stone ring wins outright. How to Play in the game has the whole of it, with the sources in the game's notes.

If something is wrong

  • The game runs slowly or the phone gets warm: Settings > Graphics > Low, and Frame rate > Battery saver. Phones with less memory start on Low by themselves.
  • The beta will not install on an older or Android Go phone: use the file ending in -32bit.apk from the beta page.
  • A word or a rule looks wrong (¿algo está mal dicho?): if you play or have watched ulama or pelota maya, please tell us; we want it right.
  • The buttons are hard to reach: Settings > Button size, Move buttons to drag them where you want them, and Left-handed to swap the sides.
