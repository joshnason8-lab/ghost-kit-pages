# Kabaddi Raid: features

- **Three modes**: Quick Match, League Season and the Nations Cup, plus a full How to Play guide.
- **League Season**: own a franchise, keep players, win the auction, pick your seven and chase the title year
  after year, with news, weekly awards, records and a hall of fame.
- **Nations Cup**: twelve countries; pick a squad of twelve and a captain and play from the groups to the final.
- **Real raiding**: hand touches, toe touches and kicks; the dubki under linked arms and the lion jump over a dive.
- **Real defending**: ankle, thigh and waist holds, dashes and chain tackles, with team-mates coming in to help.
- **Kabaddi's rules**: bonus points, revivals, all outs, do-or-die raids, super tackles, the golden raid,
  time-outs and cards, and referees signalling every call.
- **Your raid, your way**: raid on one breath of the cant, or against a 30-second clock.
- **AI that plays as a team**: defenders keep their shape and cover the lanes home; four levels from Rookie to
  Legend.
- **Five grounds**: Mumbai Dome, a village mud court at golden hour, a floodlit national stadium, a monsoon ground
  in the rain and a sand court on Puri beach.
- **Movement from real people**: motion capture (Mixamo and the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database) and
  moves studied from real match footage.
- **A crowd that reacts**: the stands murmur, cheer a point, roar at big moments and clap in time as a do-or-die
  raid starts, with real referee whistles and each ground's own rain, sea or birds.
- **Three graphics levels**: Low for modest phones, Medium and High for sharper players, courts and crowds; the
  game steps down by itself if a phone struggles.
- **Plays offline**: no internet needed, no ads, no purchases, no account and no data collected.
- **Eight languages**: English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Punjabi.
- **Controls that fit your hands**: a stick wherever your thumb lands, a left-handed layout, buttons you can move
  and resize, vibration you can turn off, and a battery saver.
