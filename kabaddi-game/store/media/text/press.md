# Kabaddi Raid: press blurb

Kabaddi Raid brings South Asia's fast, physical contact sport to Android phones in 3D. Players raid on a single
breath, touch defenders and race home, or defend with ankle, thigh and waist holds and chain tackles, under
kabaddi's real rules, from bonus points and revivals to do-or-die raids and super tackles. Three modes, Quick Match,
a franchise League Season with a player auction, and the Nations Cup, are played across five grounds, from a
village mud court to a floodlit stadium. The game plays fully offline, in eight languages, with no ads, purchases or
data collection. It is in beta now and coming soon to Google Play.
