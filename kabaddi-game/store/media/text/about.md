# Kabaddi Raid

**Raid. Touch. Return.**

## In short

Kabaddi Raid is a 3D kabaddi game for Android phones. Raid into the other half on one breath, touch a defender and
get back over the midline before they bring you down, or hold the line as a defender. Play a quick match, run a
franchise through a league season, or take a country to the Nations Cup, all offline.

## About the game

Kabaddi is one of South Asia's oldest and most loved sports: two teams of seven, one raider at a time, crossing into
the other half on a single breath to touch a defender and get home before they are brought down. It is fast, close
and physical, and Kabaddi Raid is built to play like it.

When you raid, you cross the baulk line, read the cover, and go for a hand touch, a toe touch or a kick, then turn
for home. If a defender grabs you, a dubki under linked arms or a lion jump over a dive can still get you back.
When you defend, you hold your place, wait for the raider to commit and go for the ankle, the thigh or the waist,
with team-mates joining the chain. The rules are kabaddi's own: bonus points, revivals, all outs, do-or-die raids,
super tackles, the golden raid, time-outs and cards, with referees signalling every call.

There are three ways to play. Quick Match is two teams, one ground, and a match. League Season puts you in charge
of a franchise: keep your best players, win the auction, choose your seven, and take them through the league, the
playoffs and the final, season after season, with news, weekly awards, records and a hall of fame. The Nations Cup
is a country, a squad of twelve and a captain, from the group stage to the final.

Matches are played on five grounds: an indoor arena, a village mud court at golden hour, a floodlit national
stadium, a wet mat under monsoon rain, and a sand court by the sea at Puri. A crowd watches and reacts to every
raid, and real referee whistles mark the calls.

The game plays fully offline, with no ads, no purchases, no account and no data collected. It speaks eight
languages (English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Punjabi), and has three graphics levels so it
runs on modest phones as well as new ones.
