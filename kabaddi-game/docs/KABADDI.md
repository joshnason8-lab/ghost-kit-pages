# Kabaddi rules in the game

This page lists which kabaddi rules the game plays by, which it leaves out, and where they come from. The game
follows the Pro Kabaddi League (PKL) rules, with the traditional cant as an option. Where the league and the
international rules (IKF/AKFI) differ, the difference is noted.

## In the game

| Rule | How the game plays it |
|---|---|
| Court | 10 m × 13 m, midline, baulk lines 3.75 m from it, bonus lines 1 m further, 1 m lobbies, sitting blocks behind each end line. |
| Raid | 30-second clock (PKL), or the traditional cant on one breath: by itself (Breath) or tapped on the beat (Tap). Touches score one point each; the raider must cross the baulk line. |
| 5-second rule | The raider must start the raid within 5 seconds of the whistle, or the other side gets a technical point. |
| Bonus | With 6 or more defenders on the mat, a foot over the bonus line, or a leg stretched over it with the other foot in the air. It does not revive anyone. |
| Lobby | The lobbies are part of the court only after contact. Before that, a raider who steps out is out, and the raid ends at once. This matches the PKL rule since season 9. Under the IKF rule the raid also ends, but defenders who follow the raider into the lobby are out too. |
| Out of bounds | Defenders who step or dive out before contact are out; so are defenders who cross the midline. |
| Tackles | Ankle, thigh and waist holds, block, dash and chain tackles. Two points for a tackle with 3 or fewer defenders (super tackle). |
| Revival | One player back per point scored, in the order they went out. Out players wait in the sitting block. |
| All out (lona) | Two extra points, and the whole side comes back. |
| Do-or-die | After two empty raids in a row, the third raid must score or the raider is out. |
| Time outs | Two per team per half, 30 seconds each. |
| Cards | Rough play (a shove after a tackle) can draw a card. A green card is a warning. A second offence is a yellow card: two minutes off the mat and a technical point to the other side. |
| Tie-breaker | In knockout matches: five raids each by different raiders, then golden raids, with the baulk line as the bonus line. |
| Officials | Referee, two umpires, two line judges and the scorers' table. They make hand signals: points, bonus, out, all out, time out, half time, match end and cards. |
| Milestones | Super 10 (10 raid points in a match) and High 5 (5 tackle points) are called out. |

## Officials' hand signals

There is no single official chart online, so these follow the most consistent descriptions found:

- **Points.** One arm up (fingers show the number; the game shows it as a number over the referee's head) and the other arm pointing to the scoring side.
- **Bonus.** An arm out at shoulder height toward the scoring side, thumb up.
- **Technical point.** An arm out at shoulder height, thumb down.
- **Out.** Pointing to the side whose players are out.
- **Time out.** A T made with the hands.
- **Half time.** Arms crossed in front of the chest.
- **End of the match.** Both arms straight ahead.
- **Card.** The card held up high.

## Not in the game yet

- **Substitutions.** Since season 9, PKL teams name a 14-player matchday squad, with 7 on the mat. They get 7 substitutions, plus one more at half time.
- **Reviews.** In the PKL league stage, each team gets one review per match to challenge a call.
- **Red cards.** A red card sends a player off for the match. The game stops at yellow.
- **Technical points for other offences**, such as two raiders entering at once, coaching from outside, or a raider chanting the wrong word.
- **Injury time outs.**
- **Women's court size** (12 m × 8 m) and other age-group variations.
